Word Graph — the W as five nodes in a path

The monogram's five vertices — three tops, two valleys — pulled apart into coloured balls joined by edges. The colours are the app's own distance ramp, so the W reads as a word working its way outward from a root: red at the start, blue at the end.

The balls never collide — a 2 px gap survives at 16 px, so the zigzag of five dots still reads. What dies first is the thing that makes it a letter: at 16 px the fine variant's 2.5-unit edges rasterise to 0.6 px and effectively vanish, leaving five stranded dots with no W. The heavy variant holds a 1.8 px edge and stays a letterform. Either way the colour ramp — the entire reason to do this version — is gone by 24 px, since a 5 px ball can't carry a hue.
