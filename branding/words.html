Word Graph — marks that contain words

Two ways to put the actual vocabulary into the identity. Both are self-explanatory at header size and both fall apart at tab size — which is why each pairs with a letter-free or single-letter companion.

The constraint. A three-letter run needs roughly 40 px of mark width before the glyphs stop being smudges, and a browser tab gives you 16. So "words in the logo" and "legible favicon" can't be the same asset. The usual resolution: a word-bearing mark for the app header, README, and social card, plus a letter-free icon at 16–32 px. W works as that companion because a monogram carries the brand name, where an abstract mark has to be learned (cf. the earlier chain/hub/ladder set).
